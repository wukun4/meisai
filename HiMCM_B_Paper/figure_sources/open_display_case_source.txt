The April 2021 manual covers CF-R/CF-FS-R/CF-S-R. Its printed page 4 (PDF page 8)
shows the CF-R 4-ft case external length 48.5 inches [123.2 cm], depth 30 inches
[76.2 cm], and height 57.75 inches [146.7 cm]. These match the supplied image's
rounded labels 1232 mm, 762 mm and 1467 mm. Exact inch conversions are 1231.9,
762 and 1466.85 mm, respectively. No performance values are extracted here.

The product page classifies CF as a medium-temperature refrigerated display
case for bakery/beverage/dairy/deli applications. The paper therefore uses this
figure as an illustration of open-front geometry and layered product positions,
not as a frozen-storage product specification. The internal shelf arrangement
is schematic; the manufacturer lists different standard shelf counts and
optional extra shelves. No shelf count, usable volume or shelf spacing from the
supplied illustration is treated as a verified specification.
