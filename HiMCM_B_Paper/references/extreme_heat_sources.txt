EXTREME DRY-HEAT TEST: PRIMARY SOURCES, DOMAIN AUDIT AND ASSUMPTIONS
Accessed 2026-11-08. No device performance or weather measurements are added.

1. Official SET software documentation (existing bibliography key: pythermal)
https://pythermalcomfort.readthedocs.io/en/stable/documentation/models.html#standard-effective-temperature-set
https://pythermalcomfort.readthedocs.io/en/stable/_modules/pythermalcomfort/models/set_tmp.html
The SET API describes tdb and tr applicability using 10 < temperature < 40 C,
and returns NaN outside its applicability checks when limit_inputs=True.
The package paper is Tartarini F and Schiavon S (2020), SoftwareX12,100578:
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.softx.2020.100578
The paper establishes the software method; the installed-version guard is
audited from source and execution rather than inferred from the2020 release.

Installed pythermalcomfort4.8.1 implementation audit:
pythermalcomfort/models/set_tmp.py calls _check_ashrae55_compliance when
limit_inputs=True. _internal/ashrae55.py passes (10.0,40.0) to _valid_range for
tdb and tr; _internal/validation.py rejects x<lower OR x>upper, so the endpoints
are included. Direct tests accepted Ta=Tr=40 and rejected Tr42,Ta42 and Tr44.
Thus40C is labelled a default-guard-accepted boundary, not strict-interior or
additional empirical validation. Ta=Tr=39 is the strict-interior confirmation.
All extrapolations first record default-guard rejection and its warnings;
only the separately labelled diagnostic branch then uses limit_inputs=False.
No existing backend guard or physical module is changed.

2. ASHRAE2017 Handbook--Fundamentals, Chapter9,Thermal Comfort
(existing bibliography key: thermalcomfort; source below is SI edition)
https://handbook.ashrae.org/Handbooks/F17/SI/f17_ch09/f17_ch09_si.aspx
Section3, Sensible Heat Loss from Skin, equations(5)--(6), gives convective
exchange at clothing/exposed-skin surfaces in terms of the surface-air
temperature difference. Section3, Evaporative Heat Loss from Skin,
equation(12), relates evaporation to wettedness and vapor-pressure difference.
Their signs and joint contribution explain why temperature, humidity and
air movement must enter the same heat-balance response. A fixed35C rule is
not imposed by this test, and air cooling is not substituted for SET relief.

3. Primary experimental support for competing exchanges (background source;
not used to fit parameters, and not necessary as a new paper bibliography item)
Cramer MN,Huang M,Moralez G,Crandall CG (2020). Keeping older individuals cool
in hot and moderately humid conditions: wetted clothing with and without an
electric fan. Journal of Applied Physiology128(3):604--611.
https://doi.org/10.1152/japplphysiol.00786.2019
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7099436/
Nine older participants were tested near42.4C,34.2%RH. The study quantified
concurrent changes in convective heat gain and evaporative loss. Its people,
clothing and conditions differ from our scenario; its measurements are not
transferred to our model. It supports the mechanism, not an assumed ranking
or health prediction for our40C scenario.
