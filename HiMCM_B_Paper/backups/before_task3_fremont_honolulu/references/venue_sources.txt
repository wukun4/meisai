Evidence and permitted interpretation
------------------------------------
Ullman: Official rental packet describes Ullman Terrace as an outdoor tiered patio
used for ceremonies, receptions and dining. Official October 2026 event page
names live music on Ullman Terrace, confirming a current venue. The legacy
webster-center page appeared in official search results but returned 404 when
opened; bibliography uses the official packet plus the current event page.
No outdoor area measurement is supplied by the cited sources. The separate
1,500-square-foot figure concerns Webster Auditorium; DO NOT use it as the
terrace area. Likewise 50-150 guests is a rental-complex capacity statement,
not a measurement validating our 120-guest scenario.

Satay Street: Official operator page confirms open-air alfresco dining on Boon
Tat Street, with weekday hours 7 pm-3 am and weekend hours 3 pm-3 am. The warm
afternoon scenario is consistent with weekend operation. Area and guest counts
are modeled sector assumptions, not the venue's total area/capacity. A separate
corporate-events 48-guest reservation package is not used as total capacity.

Phoenix climate: NOAA memorandum's general summary identifies a desert climate,
low annual rainfall and low relative humidity; it also describes summer monsoon
variability. It supports the dry-climate contrast, not the exact 36 C / 25% RH
design snapshot. Singapore climate: MSS describes year-round high humidity,
daily temperature variation, and daytime maxima usually around 31-33 C. Exact
32 C / 75% RH and both radiant temperatures remain assumptions.

EIA: Official table gives Arizona 2025 commercial average 12.45 US cents/kWh.
This is a statistical state-sector average, not either venue's quoted tariff.
The comparison uses a common assumed USD 0.20/kWh planning price. The 0.10-0.30
sensitivity range brackets that reference. Other ownership, maintenance and
water costs are assumptions, not manufacturer or operator quotations.

NWS heat: Official guidance supports monitoring forecasts, reducing/eliminating
or rescheduling strenuous outdoor activities to cooler times, minimizing direct
sun exposure, and cooling/rest breaks. NEA/MSE advisory uses official heat-stress
information (including WBGT), available via myENV and the weather portal, to guide
prolonged outdoor activity. The NEA page was indexed in primary-source search;
direct automated opening returned 403. No SET value is presented as a medical
threshold, and no concrete WBGT thresholds are imported into this paper.

Parameter and result audit
-------------------------
All new numerical assumptions are centralized in sections/venue_assumptions.tex
and venue_model/inputs.json. Device powers/caps, evaporation epsilon and bus
delivery profiles are imported unchanged from model/scenario.json. Dining guests
use sitting posture, with the existing 1.2 met / 0.5 clo values.

Task 3 set: all nonnegative integer per-zone (A,B,C) counts satisfying
20*x_A+30*x_B+40*x_C <= zone area; exclude the all-zero energy baseline only
when forming eta. Thus 12,166/3,374 positive-energy layouts in Phoenix/Singapore.
Full-set eta maxima: 81.0973818612 and 134.8920027428 person-C-h/kWh; tied by
A1-A5 confined to Phoenix's busiest zone and A1-A4 in Singapore's busiest zone.
A1 is shown only as the smallest tied maximizer. Task 3 common-coverage q=0.60
benchmarks minimize energy within each pure type; this is a separate matched
comparison, not an eligibility filter for the full-set eta ranking.

Task 4 set: apply q>=0.80, then group by total device counts and retain the
least-residual eligible layout. This is safe ONLY because annual energy, cost,
water and device/service-clearance loss depend on total counts, not position,
under the uniform background assumed here. The remaining 199/106 count groups
have 80/32 coverage-constrained Pareto representatives. These are NOT claimed
to be unconditional Pareto sets over all low-coverage layouts.

Decision: minimize annual cost subject to q>=.80, X<=.035,
Rbar<=4.5/7.5 C, and Eyear<=6000/2400 kWh in Phoenix/Singapore respectively.
The assumed design policies select A10C2/A10. Original-layout matrices by row:
Phoenix [[1,0,2],[5,0,0],[4,0,0]], Singapore [[4,0,0],[4,0,0],[2,0,0]].
